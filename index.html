About

I work on post-quantum cryptography and quantum computing, with a focus on making cryptographic schemes fast and secure in hardware: FPGA accelerators, secure embedded systems, and the arithmetic underneath them.

I'm a full-time research student at the University of Moratuwa, where I completed my B.Sc. Eng. (Hons) in Computer Science & Engineering in 2025, specialising in Integrated Computer Engineering (CGPA 3.90/4.0). My final-year project was a reconfigurable NTT-based polynomial multiplier for CRYSTALS-Kyber. Before that I built perception and navigation systems for robotics, and prototyped an authorization engine at WSO2.

Research interests: post-quantum cryptography, secure embedded systems, quantum computing, robotics and automation. I'm open to collaboration; feel free to get in touch.
